Hero CTA — "Plan a Trip"

Always visible in the hero area. Opens full AI chat with entity context pre-loaded. The AI already knows you're on the Cozumel page and what products are available.

System prompt includes:

"User is viewing Cozumel, Mexico (destination). Available: 42 cruises, 12 flights from YOW, 28 hotels. Basket: 1 cruise (Western Caribbean $849)."

2

Contextual Suggestion Bar

Proactive, page-aware suggestions below the pills. Changes based on what the AI notices about your browsing. Tapping expands to full chat with the suggestion as the opening message.

Trigger examples:

  • First visit: "I found 3 direct flights from Ottawa"
  • Has cruise in basket: "Your cruise stops here — need hotels for the night before?"
  • Browsed 3+ destinations: "Comparing options? I can help narrow it down"
  • Idle 30s+: "Want me to build a sample itinerary for Cozumel?"
3

Fixed Bottom Chat Input

Always accessible at the bottom of the page (mobile). Tapping opens the full chat bottom sheet. Pre-filled placeholder reflects current page context. On desktop, this becomes the "Talk to AI" nav button.

AI Context Awareness

What the AI knows
  • Current entity page (type, name, slug)
  • Available products (counts per section)
  • User's detected location (geolocation/IP)
  • Basket contents (components + prices)
  • Browsing history this session
  • Whether user is identified (email) or anonymous
What the AI can do
  • manageTripBasket — add/remove components
  • captureIdentity — collect name/email
  • Suggest products from current page data
  • Compare options across entity types
  • Build sample itineraries
  • Hand off to human advisor when complex
