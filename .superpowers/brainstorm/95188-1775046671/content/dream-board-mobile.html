A: Bottom Sheet AI

Board scrolls full-screen. AI slides up as a bottom sheet (like Google Maps). Swipe up to expand, down to minimize. Suggestions swipe up to add to board. Natural mobile gesture pattern.

C: Inline AI Bar + Chat Input

Board is always full-screen. AI shows as a compact suggestion bar at top (one suggestion at a time, swipeable). Chat input at bottom (like iMessage). Tap the bar to expand into full AI conversation. Board stays visible underneath. Most space-efficient.

Desktop Behavior (for reference)

On desktop: board fills the main area. AI panel slides out from the right, and the board resizes to accommodate (not overlays). Side-by-side when AI is open, full-width when closed.
