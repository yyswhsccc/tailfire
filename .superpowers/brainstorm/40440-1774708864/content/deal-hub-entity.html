💡 Hero Text Contrast Solution

Three layers ensure text is always readable regardless of hero image brightness:

1. Gradient scrim — Bottom-heavy dark gradient overlay (75% opacity at bottom → 15% at top)
2. Text shadow — Every text element gets text-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)
3. Bottom placement — Title/meta always positioned at the darkest part of the gradient

This works on bright beaches, white buildings, snowy mountains, and sunny ports. The gradient is strong enough to guarantee contrast but subtle enough to not make the image feel dark.
