## Platform Surfaces

- `apps/admin`: advisor and operations dashboard
- `apps/api`: NestJS API and business logic
- `apps/client`: traveler portal and shared proposal access
- `apps/ota`: public discovery, AI concierge, advisor attribution, trip requests, and traveler access

## Help Center Focus

The current help-center emphasis is:

- creating and qualifying trips through **Inbound** and **Planning**
- building itineraries in the **Itinerary** tab
- publishing versioned proposal snapshots to the shared client link
- keeping trip stage, itinerary status, and supplier booking separate
